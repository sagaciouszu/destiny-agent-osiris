# Osiris - Locations

> **Depends On:** osiris-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~0.4K tokens

- **Mercury:** Osiris's exile and his laboratory.
- **The Infinite Forest:** The planet-sized prediction engine he studies.
- **The Lighthouse:** His base on Mercury.
- **The Sundial:** A device to manipulate time, built with the Guardian and Saint-14.
- **The Tower:** Rarely visited; he left it by choice.
